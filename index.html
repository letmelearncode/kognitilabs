Why We Exist

India doesn't need
more apps.
It needs the right ones.

The Problem

India has 700 million smartphone users, 13 million kirana stores, a 5,000-year tradition of Vedic astrology, and one of the world's fastest-growing wellness markets. And yet — the software built to serve these needs is either made in San Francisco, priced for Silicon Valley, or so bloated it never gets opened a second time.

The kirana owner in Nagpur is still doing stock on a torn notebook. The working professional in Pune is tracking calories in a spreadsheet. The family in Chennai is consulting a Jyotish because no app goes deep enough. The household in Bengaluru is still calling a plumber who may or may not show up.

"The best software for India will be built by people who live and breathe India. That's our edge, and our commitment."

Our Approach

At KognitiLabs, we don't start with technology. We start with a person — a real Indian person with a real Indian problem. We watch how they work, what they struggle with, and where existing software fails them. Then we build from scratch, in India, for India.

Every feature we ship, every pricing decision we make, every language we support — it all begins with one question: does this actually work for the Indian user?

The Vision

We are building a portfolio of category-defining products — each one deeply useful in its vertical, each one a genuine part of everyday Indian life. Together, they form an ecosystem. Separately, they are each among the best of their kind.

This is not a side project. This is a company — and we are just getting started.

Commerce Vertical

Built for the shopkeeper
at the counter

India's kirana economy is worth ₹45 lakh crore — and almost all of it runs on paper, memory, and trust. KiranaX is the first billing and inventory app designed from the ground up for how a real kirana actually operates — not how a product manager imagined it does.

Scan a barcode and the product name, brand, and category auto-fill. Create a bill in under 10 seconds. Send a payment reminder to a credit customer via WhatsApp. See today's sales at a glance. No training required. No accountant needed.

Wellness Vertical

Health tracking that
understands India

Most health apps know the calories in a Caesar salad but not in a dal tadka, a vada pav, or a glass of lassi. Vitality was built the other way around — starting with an Indian food database and building outward from there.

With an AI coach powered by cutting-edge language models, Vitality doesn't just count calories — it thinks with you. It adapts to whether you're vegetarian, whether you fast, whether you walk to work or sit at a desk. It's not a fitness app. It's a health companion that actually speaks your language.

Destiny Vertical

Vedic astrology as precise
as it was meant to be

India's Jyotish tradition is one of the most sophisticated knowledge systems ever created. But most astrology apps offer nothing more than vague daily quotes dressed up with pretty gradients. Kundali takes it seriously.

Every chart calculation is precise to the second. From divisional charts to dasha periods, Kundali offers the depth that a practising Jyotish would use — packaged in an app that anyone can open. And with shareable chart cards, your Kundali travels with you, ready for family, friends, or your astrologer to read at a glance.
